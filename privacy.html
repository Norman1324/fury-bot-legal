Privacy Policy

Fury Discord Bot · Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy describes how the Fury Discord Bot processes information when used in a Discord server.

Information processed

  • Discord user, channel, server and message identifiers needed for tickets, permissions, logging and moderation.
  • Support ticket form submissions, messages and attached images used to identify and respond to technical issues.
  • Moderation events, including member joins/leaves and message edits/deletions where available.
  • Support and administrative records, including key-related support actions and associated ticket references, which may be recorded in an access-controlled Google Sheet.

How information is used

Information is processed to create and manage support tickets, produce ticket transcripts, provide troubleshooting responses (including text recognition of images), maintain server logs, prevent spam, and perform authorized moderation and support actions.

Where information is stored

Ticket transcripts and server logs may be posted in administrator-accessible Discord channels. Certain administrative support records may be stored in Google Sheets. The bot also processes data in memory while running.

Sharing

Information is accessible to authorized server staff as needed for support and moderation, and is processed by Discord and Google where their services are used. We do not claim to sell user information.

Retention and deletion

Records may remain in Discord log or transcript channels and relevant administrative sheets until deleted by authorized administrators. No fixed automatic deletion period is promised. For a request to review or delete information, contact the server administrators through the server's designated support channels. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate moderation, security or recordkeeping purposes.

Security

Access to support channels, transcript logs and administrative records is restricted by server roles and service permissions. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Updates

This policy may be updated when bot functionality or data handling changes.
